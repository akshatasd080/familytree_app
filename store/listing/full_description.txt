Family Tree helps you build a private digital home for your family.

Create or join your family, invite relatives with OTP, and grow a living family tree together. Share photos, chat with members, plan events, and see where family lives on the map — all in one secure app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Build an interactive family tree (complete tree or your circle)
• Add relatives, relationships, memorials, and member profiles
• Share a private media feed with likes & comments
• Chat 1:1 or in private family rooms
• Track birthdays, anniversaries, and custom events
• Plot family locations on a map
• Manage invitations, join requests, and family settings

PRIVACY FIRST
Your family content stays with your family. Sign in with email or mobile, verify with OTP, and control who joins.

WHO IT’S FOR
Families who want a simple place to stay connected across generations — not a public social network.
